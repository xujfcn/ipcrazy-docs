ipcrazy sells three proxy products for cross-border businesses, public-data teams and developers. You top up a USD wallet, buy the product you need in the console, and connect your software directly to the proxy entry. Proxy traffic does not pass through ipcrazy's website or console servers.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Dynamic residential" icon="arrows-rotate" href="/products/dynamic-residential">
    Traffic packages in GB. Rotating or sticky sessions over HTTP or SOCKS5.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Static residential" icon="house-signal" href="/products/static-residential">
    Fixed residential (ISP) IPs, bought per IP for 7 or 30 days.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Static native" icon="location-dot" href="/products/static-native">
    Fixed native IPs, bought per IP for 7, 30 or 90 days.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Choose a product

| Product             | Best suited to                                          | Billed by                    |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Dynamic residential | Regional testing and independent public-data requests   | Traffic package (GB, 30 days) |
| Static residential  | Long-running workflows that need a fixed exit           | IP, region and term          |
| Static native       | Workflows with specific network identity requirements   | IP, region and term          |

Not sure which one fits? The console's **Proxy advisor** asks a few questions about your task and suggests a product.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an account">
    Sign up at [ipcrazy.com](https://ipcrazy.com/dashboard) and verify your email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Top up your wallet">
    All prices, balances and orders are in US dollars.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Buy a product">
    Orders are paid from the wallet and delivered automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Generate connection details in the console and use them in your software.
  </Step>
</Steps>
