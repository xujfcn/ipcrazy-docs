Static native proxies provide fixed native IPs, selected by region and term, for authorized tasks that need a specific network identity.

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Billing | Per IP, by region and term, with quantity tiers |
| Terms | 7, 30 or 90 days |
| Delivery | Automatic after payment, per region |
| Renewal | Manual, or automatic with a confirmed price limit |

## Buying

In the console, open **Buy products → Native static**, then choose regions, a quantity per region and a term. The order is paid from your wallet in USD. IPs that cannot be delivered are refunded to your wallet automatically.

Static native has its own region list and prices, separate from static residential. A renewal order can only contain IPs of one product type.

## Assessing fit

Evaluate the route by its actual specification and by testing against your own authorized target. ipcrazy does not promise third-party anonymity ratings or IP-check scores; those results are for reference only and do not decide whether a target site will accept a connection.

After delivery, manage the IPs under **My products → Native static**. See [Static IPs](/guides/static-ips).
